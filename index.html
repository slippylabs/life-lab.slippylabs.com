RLE

The interchange format every Life catalogue uses: run-lengths of b dead and o alive, $ for end of row, ! for end of pattern. Paste anything from a pattern collection in here, or copy what you built out.

copied

Every rule here is a two-state, 8-neighbour (Moore) outer-totalistic rule, which is the family Conway's Life belongs to. A repeat of an earlier generation is detected by hashing each state, so a still life, a blinker or a period-15 oscillator names itself once it settles — a pattern that never repeats simply never triggers it.
